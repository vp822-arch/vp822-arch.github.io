Over the weekend of November 7–8 our team built an **autonomous stacking robot** at the Viam Hackathon in New York.

## What it does

- Scans a set of assorted shapes and computes an optimal packing
- Plans its grasps, then stacks and unstacks the blocks on its own
- Shows a live interface with the real-time positions of the blocks and the arm
