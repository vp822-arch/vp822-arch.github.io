At NASA Space Apps 2026 in New York, my team built **SoilSense**, a tool that recommends crop rotations to farmers. You can use it as a multilingual web app, or call a phone line if you don't have reliable internet.

## What I worked on

I led the data analysis. We ranked crop rotations for 9 Kansas districts using NASA weather and drought data, then checked whether the rankings meant anything by comparing them against USDA yield records. The correlations came out between r = 0.51 and 0.72 (p < 0.001). We also set up the data to update automatically each week.

## Result

We won the **MLH Hackathon Winner** award.
